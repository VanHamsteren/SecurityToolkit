    Quick Scan (built-in)

    The extension's own analysis, no account needed. It dissects the link (Safe Links unwrapping, base64, tracking IDs, look-alike domains) and looks up DNS, network (ASN) and domain age. The optional live fetch retrieves the page from your own connection without cookies and reads it as text only: nothing is rendered or executed.

    Contacting the target reveals your IP address and can confirm a personalised link to the sender.
    Only literal redirect targets found in the page source are followed, up to 5 extra hops.
    Live fetch needs access to all websites and to network requests (to record redirects and strip cookies). It is asked on first use if you skip this.

    Notifications and scan queue

    Progress is now shown on the results page. Everything is still logged to the browser console.
    Scan queue: 0 link(s) waiting
    Collect links during the day, scan them all at once on the queue page, then block the bad ones.
